Untethered Health Monitor

When Earth can't answer, the crew still can.

A health monitoring system for crews far from Earth. It watches each astronaut's body, the cabin air and the radiation around them; turns an abnormal reading into a clear task for the right person; and keeps checking until the data shows recovery — on board, even when advice from Earth is minutes away or cut off. Everything runs in your browser: no install, no login.

How it works: one pipeline for every health signal

  1. MeasureWearables, the medical kit, cabin sensors and short crew self-checks.
  2. Check the dataEvery reading carries its source and age. A missing or poor-contact reading is measured again, never treated as healthy.
  3. DetectThresholds from NASA standards and medical literature; every rule names its source or is marked as an assumption.
  4. AnticipateExposure history (CO₂, radiation, sleep) schedules extra self-checks before symptoms are reported.
  5. ActWritten procedures give each crew member their own task and the reason it is theirs.
  6. Confirm and reportA task closes only when the readings recover. Reports to Earth carry the time of the data; late advice is flagged.

Each signal is a sensor entry, a rule and a procedure in data files, so adding one does not change the monitoring or response code. The same code also runs a second mission, a Mars rover excursion, from its own mission pack.
