Astro Health Monitor

When Earth can't answer, the crew still can.

A crew health monitoring and response system for missions where ground advice is delayed or cut off. Sensors, wearables and crew self-checks feed one event history; the system grades readings against stated thresholds, opens written procedures, tells each person what to do and why, and keeps checking until the data shows recovery. Everything runs in your browser — no install, no login.

Decision support, not diagnosis: the medical officer decides. Crew, symptoms and equipment faults are simulated or scripted. The rover's solar particle event and the spacecraft's radiation background are real NASA measurements; the spacecraft's solar particle event is constructed from the shape of a real event (see below). Thresholds, timings and roles are project assumptions. Not clinically validated, not a medical device. No astronaut medical data is used.

What the system does not do

  • It does not diagnose, prescribe, or decide who is fit for an EVA or for duty — the crew medical officers decide; the software records their decision.
  • It cannot see inside the suit and does not measure personal dose; hand injury, decompression sickness and dust in the eyes rely on crew reports.
  • Earth support is not simulated on Mars (3–22 light-minutes one way); real-time support comes from the base.
  • Automated scenario checks show the software follows its own rules; they do not validate any medical conclusion.
